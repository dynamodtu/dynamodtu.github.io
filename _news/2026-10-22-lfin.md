Tijana Miseljic, head of DYNAMO, has joined the **Lundbeck Foundation Investigators Network (LFIN)** as
one of its 24 new members. The network brings together researchers from across the Lundbeck Foundation
community, and its new members met for the first time at a first gathering in Copenhagen (photo above). The
next gathering is in November.

> "I am very happy to share that I have joined the Lundbeck Foundation Investigators Network (LFIN) as one
> of its 24 new members! I am truly grateful for this opportunity to connect my expertise in micromotors
> with the diverse knowledge and experience of researchers across the LFIN community. I believe that
> bringing together different perspectives and expertise can open exciting new directions for our research
> and help us tackle scientific challenges together.
>
> It was a pleasure meeting so many inspiring researchers at our first gathering, and I am already looking
> forward to seeing everyone again in November!"
>
> — Tijana Miseljic
