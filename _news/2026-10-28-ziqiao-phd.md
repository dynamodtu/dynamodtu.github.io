On 23 October 2026, Ziqiao Li successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis, *Toward Active Oral Drug Delivery: From
Micromotors to Self-Catapulting Ingestible Devices*, becoming the first Ph.D. student co-supervised by Tijana
Miseljic to graduate. Ziqiao was supervised by Anja Boisen (main supervisor), Zhongyang Zhang and Tijana.
The assessment committee was Ada-Ioana Bunea, Veronica Iacovacci and Brigitte Städler.

> "Watching my first PhD student become Dr. Ziqiao Li is a moment I will always hold close to my heart. So
> proud of you, Ziqiao! 🎓
>
> On Friday, Ziqiao successfully defended his PhD, and I could not be prouder. It has been a great pleasure
> to be his co-supervisor alongside Zhongyang Zhang and main supervisor Anja Boisen.
>
> Ziqiao, working with you, exchanging ideas, and supporting your growth as a researcher have been truly
> rewarding experiences. You have demonstrated deep scientific knowledge, independence, and the ability to
> tackle challenging research questions with curiosity and determination.
>
> The sky is the limit for you! I look forward to continuing our collaboration and seeing where our next
> projects take us.
>
> A special thank you to the assessment committee, Ada-Ioana Bunea, Veronica Iacovacci and Brigitte
> Städler, for their time, thoughtful questions, and insightful discussions.
>
> Congratulations, Dr. Li! Here's to the next chapter! 🌟"
>
> — Tijana Miseljic
