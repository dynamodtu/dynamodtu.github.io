Surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) identifies molecules by their spectral fingerprints. We turn
micromotors into moving SERS sensors: their motion stirs the surrounding liquid and brings more
molecules to the sensing surface, for faster and more reliable detection of drugs and biomarkers.
This builds on world-leading SERS expertise at DTU, together with Prof. Anja Boisen.
